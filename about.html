About me

How a cybersecurity degree turned into a full-stack developer.

My story

I started out wanting to understand how systems get broken, four years into a BCA (Hons.) in Cybersecurity at Chanakya University which made me learn to think like an attacker: reconnaissance, exploitation, reporting. An internship doing real VAPT work confirmed I loved that process, but it also left me curious about the other side of the fence the people actually writing the applications I was testing.

That curiosity is why, right after graduating, I joined a Python Full Stack Development program. I wanted the skill to not just find a broken authentication flow, but to build the correct one. I'm now comfortable across Python, Django, SQL and front-end fundamentals, and I bring a security-first mindset to everything I build - input validation and access control aren't an afterthought for me, they're the first thing I check.

I'm looking for an entry-level role where that combination is useful: a Cybersecurity Analyst position, a Python Full Stack Developer role, or ideally something in between security-focused development.
